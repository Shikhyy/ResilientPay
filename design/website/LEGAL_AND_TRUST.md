# Legal, Privacy, and Trust Surfaces

> **Document role:** Normative UX/visual specification.

## Required pages

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Research Prototype Disclaimer
- Security Disclosure
- Contact

## Prototype disclaimer

The product should clearly state that the demonstration is a research prototype and that simulated balances or transactions are not evidence of real-money settlement.

## Privacy

The privacy page must explain:
- what information is collected
- why it is collected
- retention
- security
- analytics
- research data handling
- contact mechanism

Do not add analytics simply because a template includes it.

## Security disclosure

Provide a clear route for reporting suspected vulnerabilities.

The security page should not expose secrets or detailed exploit material. It should provide the reporting process and expected handling.

## Terms

Terms must not imply regulated payment capabilities that do not exist.

Legal text should be reviewed by qualified counsel before public production deployment.
