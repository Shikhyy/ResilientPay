# Brand Guidelines

> **Document role:** Normative UX/visual specification.

## Brand idea

ResilientPay represents payment infrastructure under constraint.

The identity should evoke:
- signal
- continuity
- verification
- transport
- physical proximity
- controlled recovery

## Wordmark

The wordmark should be typographic, compact, and institutional. Avoid futuristic letter distortions.

Suggested treatment:

RESPAY
or
ResilientPay

The visual system can use a small protocol marker beside the wordmark:

RESPAY / PROTOCOL RESEARCH

## Graphic motif

The primary brand motif is the Signal Line.

A Signal Line is a thin horizontal or vertical rule with explicit state markers:

CONNECTED ●──────── LOCAL ●──────── STORE ●──────── SYNC ●

It may be animated in product demonstrations, but it should remain static in most marketing content.

## Tone

Preferred:
"Payments designed for unreliable connectivity."

"Transaction integrity across degraded network conditions."

"Research prototype for bounded offline authorization and eventual reconciliation."

Avoid:
"Reinventing payments."

"Disrupting the payment industry."

"The future is here."

## Imagery

Prefer documentary, close-range technical photography:
- phones in field conditions
- NFC tags
- QR signage
- payment terminal surfaces
- connectivity test setups
- engineering benches
- network equipment

Avoid lifestyle stock imagery.
